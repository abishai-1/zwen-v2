# ZWEN — Website Purpose, Structure & Content Blueprint

## 1. Purpose of ZWEN

**ZWEN is a full-service creative technology agency that takes ideas from concept to launch.**

We combine software engineering, digital product development, mobile applications, 3D visualization, motion, branding, and creative content. Our role is not limited to designing interfaces or building frontends: we can plan, build, connect, deploy, and support complete digital products and creative experiences.

**Core positioning:** *From idea to experience.*

ZWEN helps startups, businesses, brands, and creators turn concepts into complete, usable products and compelling visual experiences.

### What ZWEN helps clients do

- Build complete web applications, from planning and interface design to backend systems, databases, APIs, deployment, and ongoing maintenance.
- Develop mobile applications from scratch, including backend integration, testing, release preparation, and deployment to the Google Play Store and Apple App Store.
- Set up and manage the infrastructure behind digital products, including servers, hosting, domains, environments, deployment pipelines, monitoring, backups, and updates where included in the project scope.
- Create 3D product visualizations, animation, motion graphics, environments, and visual effects.
- Establish brand identities and produce creative assets, social content, promotional videos, and campaigns.
- Connect technology and creativity to create cohesive digital experiences.

### Our core pillars

#### 01 — Engineering & Digital Products

- Complete web application development: frontend, backend, databases, APIs, authentication, authorization, integrations, and admin dashboards.
- Software architecture and technical planning.
- Responsive websites and web platforms.
- Mobile app development from concept to release.
- Third-party service and payment integrations where appropriate.
- Server, hosting, cloud infrastructure, domain, and environment setup.
- CI/CD and deployment workflows.
- Testing, performance optimization, security-conscious implementation, maintenance, and updates.
- Play Store and App Store release preparation and deployment, subject to platform policies, account access, and approval.

#### 02 — 3D, Motion & Visual Experiences

- 3D modeling and product visualization.
- Product animation and advertising visuals.
- Motion graphics and animated explainers.
- VFX and compositing.
- Environments and architectural visualization.
- Digital worlds and interactive visual experiences.

#### 03 — Brand, Content & Creative

- Brand identity and graphic design.
- Creative direction and visual systems.
- Social media management and content planning.
- Social media creatives and campaign assets.
- Video editing and promotional videos.
- Digital storytelling and marketing content.

The exact scope, deliverables, timelines, third-party costs, and ongoing responsibilities should be agreed upon for each project.

## 2. Website goals

The website should:

1. **Establish credibility** — present ZWEN as a capable, professional creative technology agency.
2. **Explain our scope** — make it clear that we can deliver complete products and systems, not only websites or frontend interfaces.
3. **Demonstrate execution** — show real projects, the work completed, and the thinking behind it.
4. **Communicate our range** — present engineering, mobile apps, infrastructure, 3D, branding, and content as complementary disciplines.
5. **Build trust** — introduce the people behind the agency and explain how collaboration works.
6. **Generate enquiries** — help prospective clients start a conversation about their project.

The website itself should demonstrate ZWEN's attention to design, technical execution, and user experience.

11. **Results** — verified outcomes or metrics only; do not invent numbers.

13. **Next project** — navigation to another case study.
14. **Project CTA** — invite visitors to start a related project.

## 6. Custom 404 page

**Purpose:** Help visitors recover when they visit an invalid, moved, or unavailable URL. The page should feel like part of ZWEN, not a generic browser error.

### Content

- A prominent **404** numeric display.
- Headline: **“Looks like you've entered an uncharted space.”**
- Short copy explaining that the requested page cannot be found.
- Primary CTA: **Return to Origin**.
- Secondary CTA: **Explore Selected Work**.
- Optional link to **Connect** if the visitor needs help.
- Footer with the main navigation.

### TheLab connection

The 404 page can use a subtle experimental or technical motif that nods to TheLab, but it must distinguish between a genuinely missing page and TheLab's intentional under-construction state.

- `/thelab` should show TheLab's dedicated under-construction page.
- Invalid URLs should show the custom 404 page.
- Both pages should offer clear navigation back into the main site.

### Functional requirements

- Return the correct HTTP 404 status for genuinely missing routes.
- Work on desktop and mobile.
- Keep navigation and links functional.
- Avoid redirecting every unknown URL to the homepage without explaining what happened.
- Preserve the site's visual identity and accessibility.

## 8. Site-wide requirements

### Navigation and usability

- Keep primary navigation consistent across pages.
- Make the current location and available actions clear.
- Provide obvious paths to Selected, Services, and Connect.
- Ensure menus, buttons, forms, and links work on touchscreens and with keyboard navigation.

### Responsive behavior

- Support mobile, tablet, laptop, and large desktop layouts.
- Ensure typography, interactive components, project media, and forms adapt cleanly to smaller screens.
- Avoid layouts that depend on hover alone.

### Performance and accessibility

- Optimize images, video, and 3D assets.
- Use appropriate loading strategies and avoid unnecessary heavy effects.
- Provide meaningful alternative text for informative images.
- Maintain readable contrast, keyboard access, visible focus states, and accessible form labels.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences where applicable.

### SEO and sharing

- Give each public page a meaningful title and description.
- Use appropriate heading structure and semantic markup.
- Configure social sharing metadata.
- Use canonical URLs and a sitemap where appropriate.
- Keep private, preview, and unfinished content out of search results when intended.

### Reliability and operations

- Configure production hosting and deployment carefully.
- Keep secrets and credentials out of public client-side code and repositories.
- Use appropriate error handling and logging.
- Test the contact form and other critical flows in production.
- Establish backups and monitoring where the chosen infrastructure and service scope require them.

### Content integrity

- Use real project information and approved media.
- Do not fabricate clients, testimonials, metrics, awards, or results.
- Clearly identify concept work, experiments, and ongoing projects.
- Confirm rights and permissions before publishing client work, team portraits, or third-party assets.

ZWEN's website must do more than list services. It should show that the studio can connect strategy, engineering, infrastructure, deployment, and creative execution to deliver complete solutions.
